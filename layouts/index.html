About

I am a fourth-year PhD candidate in Economics at Stockholm University, advised by Roine Vestman. My main research interests lie in household finance and public economics. I focus on the determinants of household financial behavior and how public policies and institutions shape households' financial outcomes. I am particularly interested in how cognitive abilities and limitations influence financial decisions. My work takes an applied microeconomic approach and primarily uses administrative and survey data.

In spring 2026, I was a visiting PhD student at Columbia Business School, hosted by Stephen Zeldes.
