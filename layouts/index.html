About

I am a fourth-year PhD candidate at Stockholm University, advised by Roine Vestman. My research is in household finance and public economics, with a broader interest in how households respond to financial incentives, information, and institutional design. I use administrative and survey data together with applied microeconometric methods.

In spring 2026, I was a visiting PhD student at Columbia Business School, hosted by Stephen Zeldes.

Research interests: Household Finance · Public Economics
